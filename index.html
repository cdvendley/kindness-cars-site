A simple kindness project for kids and families

Small cars bringing joy and comfort.

Started by a young patient at Seattle Children’s Hospital, the Kindness Cars Project helps create small moments of joy through kid-to-kid gifting of pocket-sized cars and toys.

The idea is simple: bring a small, clean, brand-new gift; offer it kindly; and help turn a long or stressful wait into a brighter moment.

What we do

A small gift can change the feeling of a waiting room.

Kindness Cars Project is a family-led effort to share small toy cars with children and families during hospital and clinic visits. We focus on gifts that are simple, portable, quiet, and easy to enjoy right away.

We are starting small and learning as we go. For now, this website is a short introduction for families, friends, and supporters who want to understand the project or help it grow.

If you received a car

You probably met our founder and Chief Kindness Officer.

Thanks for being part of the Kindness Cars Project. We hope a small car brought a little joy, comfort, or welcome distraction to your day.

There is nothing you need to do. But if the moment made you smile, we hope you will look for a small way to pass kindness along to someone else.

Get involved

Help create the next small moment of joy.

  • Donate brand-new Hot Wheels, Matchbox cars, or similar small toy cars.
  • Share the project with family and friends who may want to help.
  • Start your own small act of kindness when it is appropriate and welcome.
